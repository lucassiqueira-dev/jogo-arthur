🏛️ Micro Civ: Island Expansion

    A simple turn-based strategy game built in Java with an integrated web interface.

🎮 About the Game

Micro Civ: Island Expansion is a simple turn-based strategy game where you control a small civilization stranded on a procedurally generated 20×20 island map. The world consists of different terrain types like water, grass, forest, and mountains.

Each tile on the map has a type and can optionally contain a building, with some tiles providing resources depending on their terrain. Your goal is to survive and gradually expand your civilization by managing limited resources and making strategic building decisions.
🛠️ Mechanics & Buildings

    🏰 Town Center: The core of your civilization. Produces population growth and allows your territory to expand over time.

    🏠 Houses: Increase your population cap so your civilization can grow further.

    🪵 Lumber Camps: Generate wood from forest tiles.

    ⛏️ Mines / Quarries: Extract stone from mountain tiles.

    🌾 Farms: Generate food on grass tiles.

    ⚙️ Pure Strategy: No technology system or complex diplomacy—everything focuses directly on resource management and expansion.

🔁 Gameplay Loop & Win Conditions

    Gather: Automatically collect resources every turn based on your buildings.

    Build: Spend resources to construct new structures and support your economy.

    Expand: Repeat the loop until you reach a win condition (e.g., constructing a Wonder, controlling most of the island, or achieving a target population).

🚀 How to Run
Prerequisites

    Java JDK (version 17 or higher) installed on your system.
